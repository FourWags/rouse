WAGRISE — ANDROID ALARM APP 0.1.50 PREVIEW

Wake up with a dog by your side. WagRise connects a work calendar or lets you set a personal alarm. Choose from 28 illustrated dogs and mixes and 84 coat palettes; your selected companion rests on Snooze and stretches on I’m up. Its side-profile portrait becomes the actual home-screen icon. You can also choose an Original icon and color theme. The dog artwork is static, so alarm controls stay responsive and readable.

INSTALL AND UPDATE
- Install petal-wake.apk from the GitHub release, or subscribe to https://github.com/FourWags/wagrise in Obtainium. Existing Obtainium APK filters may continue to use ^petal-wake\.apk$.
- The Android app package is still app.daybreak and keeps the same signing certificate. Updating in place preserves your app data. Do not uninstall the app to update it.
- Requires Android 8 or newer with a current Android System WebView. Allow exact alarms, notifications, and lock-screen alarm display. Review Android battery restrictions and Do Not Disturb alarm access.

WORK CALENDARS
- In Calendar, choose your service and follow its sharing-link guide, or import an .ics file. Use a dedicated work-only calendar. A shared link can expose its events to anyone with the link; revoke links you no longer use.
- All-day work events need a start time set inside WagRise. Review the resulting next alarm before relying on it. Calendar providers and Android may delay updates; check after a schedule change.
- WagRise fetches feeds directly and stores links encrypted on this phone. There is no app account, analytics, advertising, or app server.

SOUNDS AND ALARM CONTROL
- Choose bundled orchestral recordings, gentle nature sounds, or standard tones. Import downloaded audio as an offline copy or link a file through Android's picker. The optional orchestra pack requires an explicit download and plays offline afterward.
- The maximum alarm volume and optional gradual rise control the phone's Android alarm stream, which other alarm apps also use. Start and end percentages are approximate because phones expose discrete volume steps. Use Test alarm to hear the actual ringing path.
- Snooze and I’m up are the two large ringing controls. I’m up requires a deliberate hold or repeated accessible selection. A snoozed alarm can be stopped from the app's active-snooze control.

APPEARANCE
- Appearance opens with dogs first. Choose a dog, view its side-profile home-screen icon and sleeping and waking artwork, then select an illustrated coat. The breed sets the actual Android launcher icon; the coat's palette colors the interface. You can hide the alarm-button art while keeping its colors and icon. There is one side-profile portrait per breed; the 84 coat variants do not recolor that portrait.
- Original designs provide 18 launcher icon choices and 19 classic color themes. Existing saved choices carry forward until a dog is selected. Choosing an Original icon afterward switches the launcher back without changing the dog artwork or its coat palette. New launcher labels say WagRise; older internal icon IDs remain so saved selections still resolve.

MOVE TO A NEW PHONE
- Create a password-protected move package in Settings. It includes calendars, future personal alarms, settings, compatible copied and linked audio, and the optional music pack if installed. Keep the package and password separately: together they can expose private calendar links. The app cannot recover the password.
- Restore only into a new or empty WagRise setup. Older Rouse move packages and Rouse/Petal Wake settings files remain accepted. Work alarms stay paused until permissions and wake times are checked on the new phone. Android permissions, an alarm already ringing, and expired personal alarms do not transfer.
- The smaller settings-only export excludes private links and alarms.

PRACTICAL LIMITS
- A powered-off or force-stopped phone cannot ring Android alarms until it is restarted or the app is opened. A feed with no visible event times cannot produce a timed work alarm without the all-day start-time rule.
- This is a personal Preview, not commercial v1 qualification. See VALIDATION.txt and the release notes for tested behavior and outstanding live-device, recovery, naming, and rights checks.
