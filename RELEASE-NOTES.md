# WagRise 0.1.53 — More dogs, more coats Preview

WagRise now offers 80 illustrated dog choices: 50 named dogs and 30 mixed companions. Each has three illustrated coats, giving you 240 coat color themes and 480 sleeping or yawning alarm poses. The chosen dog supplies a side-view home-screen icon; the selected coat colors the app and appears on the large alarm buttons. Existing dog choices and saved selections keep their IDs.

Appearance now groups named dogs and mixes and has a search field. Shiba Inu is first; the remaining recognized breeds follow the [AKC's 2025 U.S. registration ranking](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/news/most-popular-dog-breeds-2025/). Goldendoodle and mixes are unranked. The Pomeranian includes a deliberately scruffy, sleepy black-and-tan look. Original colors have their own selector without dog coats. Original icons and the basic-look reset remain available. Each dog still has one side-profile home-screen portrait; coat choices color the app and alarm artwork rather than recoloring that portrait. The Android package, signing certificate, and stable APK filename remain the same for in-place updates and Obtainium.

This is a personal Preview, not commercial v1 qualification. The exact signed APK passed 44 fresh JVM tests, three targeted Android 15 emulator checks, the full 240-theme/480-pose decode and contrast check, and an in-place Pixel installation with the same signer. The Pixel installation preserved its original first-install date, exact-alarm access, and phone alarm-volume setting. No audible Pixel alarm or live calendar inspection was performed for this update.
