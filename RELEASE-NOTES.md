# WagRise 0.1.55 — Clearer dog picker Preview

The Appearance screen now gives its two dog-finding controls distinct labels: **Search by name** filters the list, and **Browse all dogs** opens the full dropdown. Its empty prompt is simply **Select…**. The repeated instruction above the picker has been shortened. After choosing a dog, its three coat choices still appear below. Shiba Inu remains first, followed by AKC-ranked breeds, then unranked Goldendoodle and mixes. Original color themes remain in their own selector.

The Android version number advances again so Obtainium offers this final wording build to devices that installed an earlier 0.1.54 candidate.

This is a personal Preview. The exact signed APK passed 44 fresh JVM tests and the dog picker, launcher-icon, and alarm-artwork checks on an isolated Android 15 emulator. The final wording build has not yet been installed on the owner's Pixel because it disconnected from ADB; an earlier 0.1.54 candidate installed in place with the same certificate and preserved the original first-install date, exact-alarm access, and phone alarm-volume setting. No audible alarm or live calendar content was used for this wording update. See `CHECKS.txt` for the measured results and remaining v1 limits.
